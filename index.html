OUR STORY

Twelve years at the heart of the old town

Started as a vintage clothing shop that happened to serve good coffee, Konrad evolved into one of Luxembourg's most-loved café-bars — eclectic, multicultural, and completely unpretentious. Sofas from another era, a basement stage for live music and comedy, and the carrot cake that made this place famous with locals, expats, and tourists alike. Open 365 days a year, morning coffee to evening aperitif.
